# SurroSim v0.2 — Validated FEA engine

v0.2 turns the v0.1 geometry foundation into an automated CalculiX
static-structural pipeline. The engine has been run end-to-end and validated
against Euler-Bernoulli beam theory; evidence lives in
`reports/validation/`.

Verified on Windows with CalculiX 2.23 and Gmsh 4.15.2 (Python 3.12).

## Implemented

- Strict ASCII Gmsh MSH 2.2 parser retaining `SOLID`, `FIXED_FACE` and
  `LOAD_FACE` physical groups. Supported element families: 3-node triangles
  (type 2), 6-node quadratic triangles (type 9), 4-node tetrahedra (type 4)
  and 10-node quadratic tetrahedra (type 11).
- CalculiX `.inp` generation for `C3D4` and `C3D10` solids, including the
  Gmsh → CalculiX quadratic-tet node-ordering permutation.
- Fixed boundary condition on `FIXED_FACE`.
- Requested force interpreted as a **total force** and distributed to
  `LOAD_FACE` nodes; mesh refinement therefore does not multiply the total
  force. Quadratic faces use the consistent uniform-traction load vector
  (midside nodes carry the load; corners carry none).
- Hole-local mesh grading (`Distance` + `Threshold` Gmsh fields) so the
  stress concentration is resolved without refining the whole plate.
- CalculiX runner with machine-readable statuses: `SUCCESS`,
  `SOLVER_NOT_FOUND`, `SOLVER_TIMEOUT`, `SOLVER_FAILED`, `OUTPUT_MISSING`.
- ASCII FRD parser for the last `DISP` and `STRESS` nodal result blocks.
- Displacement magnitude and Von Mises calculation from
  `SXX,SYY,SZZ,SXY,SYZ,SZX`.
- Hole-region stress target (`1.5 × hole diameter`) with the first 10% of
  plate length excluded from ROI consideration.
- `run.json` per case and `fields.csv` containing nodal coordinates,
  displacement, stress components and Von Mises stress.
- No-hole geometry is allowed for the analytical cantilever validation.
- `surrosim validate beam` and `surrosim validate mesh` studies that write
  machine-readable reports plus plots.

## Commands

```bash
surrosim generate --out outputs/plate_0001
surrosim prepare --out outputs/plate_0001
surrosim simulate --out outputs/plate_0001 --ccx ccx
surrosim simulate --hole-refinement-mm 1.2 --out outputs/plate_0001 --ccx ccx
surrosim validate beam --out outputs/validation --ccx ccx
surrosim validate mesh --out outputs/validation --ccx ccx --hole-refinement-mm 1.2
```

`simulate` returns non-zero when the solver or result stage is unsuccessful
and records the failure in `run.json`.

## Validation evidence

### Beam validation — `reports/validation/beam/report.md`

Unperforated cantilever (L=120 mm, W=40 mm, T=6 mm, F=500 N, steel) against
Euler-Bernoulli theory.

| element | mesh (mm) | max disp error (%) | mid-span outer-fiber SXX error (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C3D4 | 1.5 | -19.3 | -11.1 |
| C3D10 | 4.0 | -2.57 | +0.29 |
| C3D10 | 2.0 | -2.39 | +0.24 |
| C3D10 | 1.5 | -2.35 | +0.64 |

- C3D4 is unacceptable for bending at practical mesh sizes; C3D10 is the
  production element.
- C3D10 tip displacement converges to -2.35% versus theory and the gap
  shrinks to -1.37% at L/T=40. The residual is the known stiffening from a
  fully clamped 3-D end face and the distributed end traction — not a
  discretization error (the change between 2.0 mm and 1.5 mm is 0.03%).
- The bending stress field away from the ends agrees with beam theory to
  better than 0.7%, including at the mid-span station where the hole ROI
  stress is defined.

### Mesh convergence — `reports/validation/mesh/report.md`, `reports/validation/mesh_refined/report.md`

Perforated production geometry (d=12 mm at mid-span):

| policy | mesh (mm) | ROI stress (MPa) | ROI change (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| uniform | 4.0 | 259.09 | — |
| uniform | 3.0 | 265.92 | +2.64 |
| uniform | 2.0 | 269.26 | +1.26 |
| uniform | 1.5 | 267.01 | -0.84 |
| hole graded 1.2 mm | 3.0 | 269.75 | — |
| hole graded 1.2 mm | 2.0 | 270.46 | +0.26 |
| hole graded 1.2 mm | 1.5 | 270.83 | +0.14 |

Uniform meshes leave the peak nodal stress oscillating at the ±1% level
because the hole surface is under-resolved; grading the hole surface to
`d/10` makes the target converge monotonically and remain within 0.15%
between the two finest meshes.

## Production mesh policy

- Element order 2 (`C3D10`).
- Hole surface graded to `min(0.75 × global mesh size, hole diameter / 10)`
  with grading distance `1.5 × hole diameter`.
- Global mesh size is a dataset-generation policy decision recorded per case
  in `metadata.json` / `run.json`.

## Known limitations

1. Linear elasticity, small strain, single static transverse load case.
2. Peak stress at the hole depends on nodal sampling; the policy above
   bounds that sensitivity to ~0.15% but it is a discretized peak, not an
   averaged or extrapolated stress.
3. Stress fields near the clamped and loaded faces contain expected
   constraint/load-introduction concentrations; the ROI excludes the clamp
   region.
4. No contact, plasticity, dynamics, thermal or modal analysis.

No ML label should be published without preserving the per-case provenance
(`run.json`, mesh metadata, solver status and runtime) produced by this
engine.
